- A new European Commission report confirms Europe's rapid aging due to falling birthrates and longer lifespans, projecting that nearly one-third of EU residents will be over 65 by mid-century. This demographic shift places immense strain on social welfare systems and shrinks the working-age population. The trend makes immigration economically crucial, yet political debates often favor austerity, which risks fueling far-right populism and unfairly burdening younger generations with a broken social model.

- UK homes, built for a historically mild climate, are becoming dangerously hot in summer, lacking features like proper insulation or shutters. As heatwaves intensify, the failure of government retrofitting schemes and a lack of a national strategy are pushing homeowners towards energy-intensive air conditioning, which exacerbates the urban heat island effect. The editorial argues for urgent, systemic changes in building standards and urban planning to adapt to climate change, despite significant funding challenges.
